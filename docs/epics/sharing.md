# Epic 12: Sharing

## Status

➖ Superseded on 2026-11-06. Sharing a list was planned for phase 2 of Tasks; Tusk's workspaces and boards replace it, in phase 1 (epics 16, 17 and
18). The task IDs below are kept so that they are never reused.

## Overview

Tasks planned to let a person share one list with other accounts, with an owner, members and assignment, after an RFC. Tusk shares through workspaces
instead: a person's Desk stays theirs alone, and shared work lives on boards, with roles, visibility, assignees and an Inbox (`DESIGN.md` §7.2, §13 to
§16). Nothing of this epic was built.
